Usage information

Trading Robot Storage is an internal utility operated by its owner, not a public hosted service. It copies selected archive files and verifies restored content. It does not offer storage subscriptions, public account registration, investment advice, or trading execution.

Its Google OAuth access is limited to the drive.file permission described in the privacy notice. The operator retains control of Google authorization, source files, and archive retention. A successful upload alone is not permission to delete a locally required source.
